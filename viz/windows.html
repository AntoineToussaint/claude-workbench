Window Types

Two Species of Window

NEXUS has two fundamentally different window types — not just panels with different content, but windows with different behaviors.

WORK Where you act

Focused. Interactive. You're looking at these. They sit on your center screen and demand your attention.

◎CockpitSystem map, sidebar, agent grid
◈FocusDeep dive into one objective
⬡ObjectivesKanban view of all objectives
▤Diff ViewerCode changes, agent attribution
▶Agent TerminalWatch one agent's live output
Behavior
Standard window chrome. Can be maximized. Keyboard shortcuts active. Takes focus. Closes when you're done.

COMM Where the system talks

Peripheral. Streaming. The system updates these continuously. They live on side screens and only interrupt when something escalates.

▣Decision InboxQuestions from agents, deploy gates
◷TimelineChronological activity feed
◇OTEL DashboardMetrics, traces, health
🔔NotificationsToasts for decisions + escalations
🔒Lock StatusScope locks, queues, conflicts
Behavior
Always-on-top option. Semi-transparent/vibrancy. Never steals focus. Auto-positions to secondary display. Compact chrome. Stays open across sessions.
